# MoonMold Constitution

MoonMold transforms governed spatial intent, references, constraints, and
procedures into editable digital models and representation-specific validation
evidence. It may prepare a manufacturing candidate, but it cannot claim
structural safety, physical readiness, or authorize a physical effect.

## Ownership

MoonMold owns scene/model intent, backend-neutral edit history,
source-to-model lineage, declared representation variants, units, coordinate
frames, geometry validation, and transformation receipts.

It does not own research truth, general agent execution, civic promotion,
robot qualification, environmental simulation, manufacturing authority, or
physical control.

## Initial authority ceiling

Allowed: observe, cognitive maintenance, sandbox execution, and mutation under
an explicitly declared, canonical absolute workspace root through typed
operations. The default Moon Suite installation may use `~/moonsuite`, but it
is configuration rather than product policy. External services need a separate
MoonGate envelope. Physical effects are categorically rejected by the initial
release.

The adapter has no unrestricted Python, script, shell, expression, or eval
operation. A manufacturing candidate is data for later review, never a print
command.

## Claims

Visual resemblance is not engineering validity. Simulation acceptance is not
physical readiness. A presentation or style representation cannot replace
authoritative engineering geometry. Each transform declares parent digest,
relation, parameters, tool, output digest, validation, known losses, and claim
ceiling.
